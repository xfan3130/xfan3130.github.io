TOTAL RECALL

A free-for-all memory showdown. Five rounds, each one makes your brain do something strange, then shows you what happened and why.

2–6 players + 1 host≈ 45 minutesAges 13+AP Psychology · Unit 2: Cognition

Goal

Score the most points across five memory rounds. Everyone plays for themselves. There are no teams.

What's in the box

  • The game screen (this website), shown on a laptop or projector. It flashes the cards, runs the timers and buzzers, and keeps score.
  • Recall sheets, one per player (printed on the last page).
  • Pencils, one per player.
  • A host, one person who runs the laptop. The host can also play, but should not look at the answer key early.

Setup (2 minutes)

  1. Open the game and press New Game.
  2. Choose the number of players and type in names. Each player is given a color and a buzzer key: Q Z T B P M (or number keys 1–6, or tap your name on screen).
  3. Pick Deck A or Deck B. If your group has played before, use the other deck so the lists are new.
  4. Hand out a recall sheet and pencil to each player. Sit where everyone can see the screen.

How every round works

Each round has the same four steps:

  1. Play. The screen flashes words or strings. Players follow the round's instructions.
  2. Answer key. The host reads the answers aloud. Players raise a hand for each one they got, and the host taps that card on screen once per raised hand. This builds a chart of your group's results.
  3. GOTCHA! The screen reveals the psychology concept you just experienced, with your group's chart and the key terms.
  4. Score. Swap sheets with the player on your left and grade them. The host enters each player's points.

Important: don't read ahead or tell other players what a round is "really" testing. The game only works if your brain is surprised!

The five rounds

1 pt per word · max 15

Round 1: The List

15 words flash one at a time. Watch without writing. When the timer starts, write as many words as you can remember in the Round 1 box, in any order.
1 pt per string · max 6

Round 2: Strings

Six strings of numbers or letters flash one at a time, each longer than the one before. After each one disappears, write it on the next line of the Round 2 box. It must be exact, in the right order.
1 pt per word · max 12

Round 3: Snap Judgments

Each card shows a word with a yes/no question ("Could you eat this?", "Is it in CAPITAL letters?"). Shout YES or NO as fast as you can. Keep your pencils down. The screen will tell you how this round is scored at the end.
1 pt per answer · max 8

Round 4: Memory Check

15 words flash. Then 8 test words appear one at a time. For each, write Y (it was on the list) or N (it wasn't) on lines 1–8 of the Round 4 box.
3 / 2 / 1 pts · −1 if wrong

Round 5: Recall Bowl (buzzer round)

12 toss-up questions about every earlier round. Anyone can buzz at any time. The first player to buzz answers out loud within 5 seconds, and the host presses Correct or Wrong.
Question typeExamplePoints
Free recall"What was the FIRST word on the Round 1 list?"3
Cued recall"A word from Round 3: B _ _ _ _ _"2
Recognition"Which was on the Round 1 list? A, B, C or D?"1
A wrong answer costs −1 and locks you out of that question, and the others can still buzz. If nobody knows, the host presses Reveal.

Winning

After Round 5, the player with the most total points wins. Ties are broken by Round 5 score, then by a single sudden-death question chosen by the host.

Sample of play

The words below are made up for this example. They are not the real lists.

Round 1. Maya, Jordan and Sam watch these cards flash by:

1 / 15lantern
2 / 15ocean
… 15 / 15trophy

The 60-second timer starts. Maya writes: lantern, ocean, cookie, trophy, falcon, pillar.

The host reads the list aloud. For "lantern," all 3 players raise a hand, so the host taps lantern 3 times. For "orbit" (word #8), nobody raises a hand, so it gets 0 taps.

GOTCHA! The chart forms a "U": the first and last words were remembered best. That's the serial position effect.

Maya swaps sheets with Jordan. Jordan counts 6 correct words on Maya's sheet, so the host enters 6 for Maya.

Round 5. The screen shows "A word from Round 1: O _ _ _ _" (Cued recall, 2 pts). Sam presses T and Sam's name lights up.

Sam says "orbit." That's wrong, so Sam gets −1 and is locked out. Jordan buzzes with Z and says "ocean." That's correct, so Jordan gets +2.

Host tips

  • Pause stops any timer. Everyone's done skips the rest of it.
  • Tallying hands is optional but makes the GOTCHA charts much better. It takes about 1 minute per round.
  • The Menu button (top right) quits the game.
  • Short on time? Set the recall timer to 45s and skip the tallies. The game still fits in 30–35 minutes.
